The short version

This house is listed at $749,000 after a $50,000 price cut, and it's been sitting for 78 days. The sellers updated it, but they didn't update it well. Even though it's updated, most buyers who walk through are still going to want to rip out the finishes.

I think someone could pay $675,000 to $700,000 for it in today's market. To be clear, I'm not recommending you pay the top of that range. For you, $675,000 would be a reasonable purchase and anything in the $625,000 to $650,000 range would be a great one.

Where I land

The Spruce sale at $750,000 has more sensible finishes. The Nichols sale at $627,000 is right across the street and still factors in heavily. The pending house on the same street has more finished space and is likely to close under $730,000.

Put that together with the small yard, the finishes, the unfinished basement, the landscaping, and the layout, and I expect this house to trade somewhere around $675,000 to $700,000. The $835,000 Tamarac sale shows what this same layout can do with a full remodel, and that's the dream scenario, not the starting point. I'm not recommending you go to $700,000. $675,000 would be a reasonable price for you, and if we can get it in the $625,000 to $650,000 range, that's a great buy.
